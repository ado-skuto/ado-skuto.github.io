# Visual AI Illustration Prompts

This file defines the visual style for all generated illustrations.

Use it for service panels and questionnaire steps.

Do not use generated illustrations as the hero coach image.

## Core visual style

Generate an artistic hand-drawn sports sketch.

The illustration should contain:

- simplified stick-like human anatomy
- recognizable exercise pose
- irregular unfinished outlines
- visible construction lines
- cross-hatching
- loose movement marks
- imperfect geometric background shape
- controlled asymmetry
- limited cream, blue and brown palette

The result should feel like a professional editorial fitness drawing.

It must not look childish, photorealistic or like a polished vector mascot.

The figures can be stickman-like, but the execution should feel intentional, athletic and art-directed.

## Master prompt

Editorial hand-drawn fitness illustration of [SUBJECT].

Show [POSE OR ACTION] using simplified stickman-like human anatomy.
The body position must be immediately understandable and physically plausible.

Style: professional editorial sports sketch, not a children's cartoon.
Use irregular ink outlines, partially unfinished lines, visible construction
strokes, loose cross-hatching and subtle movement marks. The drawing should
look like an intentional art-directed training sketch, not a mascot or clip art.

Use a controlled asymmetrical composition with one imperfect abstract
background shape. Keep the figure simple, lean and athletic. Avoid detailed
facial features and avoid exaggerated muscles.

Color palette: midnight blue, dusty blue, camel brown and warm cream.
Use flat color sparingly. Keep most of the drawing as line work and hatching.

Professional contemporary sports editorial aesthetic.
Minimal facial detail. No logo, watermark or photographic background.
Clean negative space. Suitable for a premium personal trainer website.

Aspect ratio: [ASPECT RATIO].
Background: [TRANSPARENT OR WARM CREAM].

## Negative prompt

photorealistic, realistic skin, 3D render, glossy vector, childish cartoon,
comic book, anime, mascot, clip art, exaggerated muscles, detailed face,
perfect smooth lines, neon colors, gym advertisement, logo, watermark,
busy background, multiple unrelated people, malformed hands, extra limbs

## Gemini copy pattern

Copy the master prompt into Gemini and replace the bracketed variables.

Add one or two short sentences under it for the specific asset, for example:

Use a transparent background.
This image will appear beside a questionnaire step about training experience.

## Asset variables

For each generated asset define:

- SUBJECT
- POSE OR ACTION
- ASPECT RATIO
- BACKGROUND
- intended page location

## Recommended ratios

- Service panel: 4:3
- Desktop questionnaire side image: 3:4
- Mobile questionnaire image: 4:3
- Decorative section image: 1:1

## Example subjects

- trainer assisting a squat
- athlete performing a plank
- person training with resistance bands
- person preparing a workout plan
- runner stretching before training
- athlete checking progress
- relaxed figure after completing a workout

## Consistency check

Reject an image when:

- the exercise is unclear
- anatomy is implausible
- lines are too polished
- colors fall outside the palette
- the style resembles a children’s cartoon
- the background is visually busy
- the image contains text
