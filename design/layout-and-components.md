# Layout and Components

## Global layout

Use a centered content container:

```css
max-width: 1200px;
padding-inline: clamp(20px, 5vw, 64px);
```

Sections should have generous vertical spacing.

Allow decorative elements to exceed the container, but not interactive content.

## Hero

Use a large asymmetrical composition containing:

- coach branding
- headline
- supporting text
- primary CTA
- real photograph of the coach

The coach photo is the dominant visual element.

Place it over an irregular camel or dusty-blue shape.

Thin unfinished lines or hatching may overlap its edges.

The headline may slightly overlap the photo, but never the coach's face.

On mobile, show text first and the photo below it.

## Buttons

Primary button:

- navy background
- near-white text
- 8px radius
- optional arrow icon
- minimum height 44px

Secondary button:

- transparent background
- navy border
- navy text
- same size and shape as primary

Buttons should move upward by 2px on hover.

Do not use gradients, glossy effects or pill shapes.

## Services

Show exactly three services:

- Osobný tréning
- Online coaching
- Zostavenie tréningového plánu

Do not display service numbers.

Each panel contains:

- service title
- short description
- illustration
- CTA opening its questionnaire

Panels may use different compositions, but CTA placement must remain predictable.

Use cream, near-white and pale-blue surfaces.

Illustrations may cross panel boundaries slightly.

On mobile, stack all panels vertically.

## Testimonials

Use screenshots from Messenger, WhatsApp or similar applications.

Arrange screenshots along a shallow curved path.

The active screenshot is:

- centered
- largest
- fully opaque
- above neighboring screenshots

Neighboring screenshots are:

- smaller
- slightly rotated
- partially hidden
- vertically offset

Place short overlay text beside or partly across the screenshot edge.

Do not cover important screenshot content.

Use visible previous and next arrow buttons.

Clicking a neighboring screenshot should activate it.

## Footer

Include:

- email
- Instagram
- YouTube

Use recognizable icons.

Keep the footer visually simple.

Use either a navy background with cream text or cream with navy text.

A cropped illustration or hatched shape may extend into one corner.
